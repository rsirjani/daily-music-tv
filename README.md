# Daily Music TV screen

A Google Cast receiver page for a home music setup: while an album plays, the TV shows a
slowly drifting wallpaper made from the album art, with a small card in the corner for the
song, artist, album and time.

The music and the album art come from a helper on the home network; this page only draws
the screen. It holds no addresses, keys or other settings.

To preview the look in any browser, open `receiver.html?demo`. Optional:
`&art=<image url>&title=...&artist=...&album=...&paused`.
